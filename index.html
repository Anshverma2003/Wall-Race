How to play
  • Each player starts in the middle of their own edge. Who moves first is decided at random.
  • On your turn, either move your pawn one square (up, down, left or right) or place a wall between two dots.
  • Walls block movement for both players. A wall can never cut a player off completely — there must always be a path to the goal.
  • If the pawns are face to face you may jump over your opponent. If a wall is behind them, step diagonally around instead.
  • The first player to reach the opposite edge wins instantly.
  • Want to practise? Play vs AI runs entirely in your browser. The AI is a game-tree search, not a chatbot.
